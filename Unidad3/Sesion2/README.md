# Unit 4 – Session 2: Read Quality Control

## Introduction

This session focuses on **RNA-seq read quality control**, a fundamental step in ensuring the reliability of subsequent analyses. The quality of raw reads is evaluated, and potential technical problems are identified.

--

## Objectives

- Evaluate the quality of RNA-seq reads.

- Identify common problems in sequencing data.

- Interpret quality control metrics and reports.

--

## Content

- Read quality analysis.

- Review of metrics and reports.
