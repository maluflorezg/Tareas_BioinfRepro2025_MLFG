Unit 2 – Session 3: Population Statistics and Structure

## Introduction

The third session of Unit 2 focuses on the use of **population statistics and genetic structure models** to analyze relationships between populations. Building on previous exploratory data and analyses, methods are incorporated that allow for the formal evaluation of similarities, differences, and possible genetic admixture events between populations.

--

## Objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

- Understand the use of population statistics for the analysis of genetic data.

- Apply metrics that evaluate relationships and differences between populations.

- Interpret results from population structure models.

- Integrate numerical and graphical results into a coherent discussion.

- Present conclusions clearly and reproducibly.

--

## Content

The following topics are covered in this session:

### 📐 Population Statistics

- Introduction to statistics used in population genetics.

- Evaluation of genetic relationships between populations.

- Biological interpretation of the values ​​obtained.

### 🧬 Genetic Structure Models

- Use of tools to infer population structure.

- Comparison between models and evaluation of their fit.

- Discussion of hypotheses about population history and genetic admixture.
