# Unit 2 – Session 2: Exploratory Analysis of Genetic Data

## Introduction

This session covers methods of **exploratory analysis in population genetics**, with the aim of identifying patterns of genetic variation among individuals and populations. Using the genetic data reviewed in the previous session, statistical and visual techniques are applied to summarize highly dimensional information and intuitively explore relationships between samples.

--

## Objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

- Apply exploratory analysis methods to population genetic data.

- Understand the use of **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** in population genetics.

- Visualize and describe clustering patterns among individuals or populations.

- Interpret graphs and results obtained from genetic data.

- Document analyses and results clearly and reproducibly.

---

## Content

This session will cover the following topics:

### 📊 Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

- Fundamentals of PCA applied to genetic data.

- Dimensionality reduction to summarize genetic variation.

- Graphical representation of individuals and populations in the first principal components.

### 🔎 Exploration and Interpretation of Results

- Identification of genetic clusters and gradients.

- Comparison between populations or groups of samples.

- Biological discussion of the observed patterns.
