# Unit 4 – Session 3: Analysis and Exploration of Results

## Introduction
In this session, you will analyze and explore the results obtained from the processed data. You will generate visualizations and interpret gene expression patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze processed gene expression data.

- Generate exploratory graphs and visualizations.

- Interpret the results obtained from the analysis.
